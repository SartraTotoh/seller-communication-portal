Seller Communication Portal v4.9.3R1
Hosting Verification Resume - Data Safe

Root cause addressed:
The original v4.9.3 flow treated a short read-back verification window on the web.app origin as a deployment failure even after Firebase Hosting CLI had returned successfully.

R1 recovery behavior:
1. Read-only verification of web.app and firebaseapp.com.
2. Cache-busting on every request.
3. Invoke-WebRequest plus curl.exe fallback.
4. 60-second read-only propagation window before any write.
5. If still not visible, exactly one Firebase Hosting-only deploy.
6. 90-second two-origin verification after deploy.
7. No Apps Script deployment/update.
8. No setup action.
9. No Google Sheets write.
10. No Smart Link repair/migration.

Existing Smart Link records are therefore outside this recovery script's write surface.
